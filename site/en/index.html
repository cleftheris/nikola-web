The practice

Meet Nikola Papageorgopoulou

Nikola Papageorgopoulou is a dental surgeon and graduate of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. Her clinical experience began at the General Hospital of Athens and continued in collaboration with Dr Nikos Krompas’ renowned Dental Place centre.

Since 2008 she has run her own modern practice in Argyroupoli, equipped with an intraoral scanner and digital radiography for precise, pain-free treatment. As a mother herself, she created a dedicated kids’ corner so that visiting the dentist is a pleasant, familiar experience for the whole family.

  • Graduate of the School of Dentistry, University of Athens
  • Clinical experience at the General Hospital of Athens
  • Collaboration with Dental Place (Dr Nikos Krompas)
  • Strict hygiene and sterilisation standards

Services

Complete dental care in one place

Every treatment is carried out by the dentist with the relevant specialty, with respect for your time and comfort.

  • General & Preventive Dentistry

    Check-ups, professional cleaning, fillings and personalised oral-hygiene advice for healthy teeth that last.

    with Nikola Papageorgopoulou

  • Paediatric Dentistry

    A gentle, playful approach and a dedicated kids’ corner, so children feel at ease and happy to cooperate.

    with Nikola Papageorgopoulou

  • Dental Implants

    Stable, natural-looking replacement of missing teeth with modern implant systems and careful planning.

    with N. Papageorgopoulou and Dr M. Bateli

  • Cosmetic Dentistry

    Whitening, veneers and aesthetic restorations for a bright, natural smile that suits you.

    with N. Papageorgopoulou and Dr M. Bateli

  • Orthodontics & Invisalign

    Invisalign clear aligners and modern orthodontic methods for children and adults — crowding, crossbite and early prevention.

    with Dr Angeliki Karagkiolidou

  • Periodontics

    Prevention and treatment of gingivitis and periodontitis, plus oral surgery, for healthy gums and stable teeth.

    with Dr Maria Bateli

  • Endodontics

    Root canal treatment, retreatment and microscopic endodontics to save your natural tooth — without pain.

    with Maria Markou, MSc

  • Prosthodontics

    Crowns, bridges and veneers that precisely restore both function and the aesthetics of your smile.

    with Dr Maria Bateli

  • Digital Dentistry

    Intraoral scanning and digital radiography: more accurate diagnosis, lower radiation and no uncomfortable impressions.

    for every treatment
